# 003 — Trigonometry and complex numbers

**Paper:** Mathematics optional, foundation bridge for both papers.

**Curriculum entry:** [Phase A, chapter 003](Mains-Mathematics-Curriculum.md#phase-a--orientation-and-selective-bridges).

**Authored:** 24 November 2026. All three parts below contain teaching material.

**Prerequisites:** [002 — Algebra, inequalities and functions](002-algebra-inequalities-and-functions.md): equations, factorisation, square roots, domains, ranges and inverse functions. No calculus or previous complex-number knowledge is assumed.

This chapter connects angles and circular motion, exponential growth, and points in the complex plane. Keep track of domains and of how many answers a representation allows. An inverse sine returns one angle; a trigonometric equation can have infinitely many. A nonzero complex number has infinitely many arguments, but exactly one in a specified principal interval.

## Paper I

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let A = [[2, 2], [1, 3]]. Find a non-singular matrix P such that P^{−1}AP is a diagonal matrix.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Show that similar matrices have the same characteristic polynomial.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Integrate the function f(x, y) = xy(x^{2} + y^{2}) over the domain R : {−3 ≤ x^{2} − y^{2} ≤ 3, 1 ≤ xy ≤ 4}.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Find the equation of the tangent plane at point (1, 1, 1) to the conicoid 3x^{2} − y^{2} = 2z.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Find the shortest distance between the skew lines:
(x − 3)/3 = (8 − y)/1 = (z − 3)/1 and (x + 3)/(−3) = (y + 7)/2 = (z − 6)/4.

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the volume of the solid above the xy-plane and directly below the portion of the elliptic paraboloid x^{2} + y^{2}/4 = z which is cut off by the plane z = 9.

**(b) [15 marks]**

A plane passes through a fixed point (a, b, c) and cuts the axes at the points A, B, C respectively. Find the locus of the centre of the sphere which passes through the origin O and A, B, C.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Show that the plane 2x − 2y + z + 12 = 0 touches the sphere x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} − 2x − 4y + 2z − 3 = 0. Find the point of contact.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Suppose U and W are distinct four dimensional subspaces of a vector space V, where dim V = 6. Find the possible dimensions of subspace U ∩ W.

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Consider the matrix mapping A : R^{4} → R^{3}, where A = [[1, 2, 3, 1], [1, 3, 5, −2], [3, 8, 13, −3]]. Find a basis and dimension of the image of A and those of the kernel A.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Prove that distinct non-zero eigenvectors of a matrix are linearly independent.

**(c) [15 marks]**

If f(x, y) = xy(x^{2} − y^{2})/(x^{2} + y^{2}), (x, y) ≠ (0, 0);
f(x, y) = 0, (x, y) = (0, 0),
calculate ∂^{2}f/∂x∂y and ∂^{2}f/∂y∂x at (0, 0).

**(d) [10 marks]**

Find the locus of the point of intersection of three mutually perpendicular tangent planes to ax^{2} + by^{2} + cz^{2} = 1.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Reduce the following equation to the standard form and hence determine the nature of the conicoid: x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} − yz − zx − xy − 3x − 6y − 9z + 21 = 0.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Consider the following system of equations in x, y, z:
x + 2y + 2z = 1
x + ay + 3z = 3
x + 11y + az = b.
(i) For which values of a does the system have a unique solution?
(ii) For which pair of values (a, b) does the system have more than one solution?

**(c) [10 marks]**

Examine if the improper integral ∫_{0}^{3} 2x dx/(1 − x^{2})^{2/3} exists.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Prove that π/3 ≤ ∬_{D} dx dy/√(x^{2} + (y − 2)^{2}) ≤ π where D is the unit disc.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Find the differential equation representing all the circles in the x-y plane.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Suppose that the streamlines of the fluid flow are given by a family of curves xy = c. Find the equipotential lines, that is, the orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves representing the streamlines.

**(c) [10 marks]**

A fixed wire is in the shape of the cardioid r = a(1 + cos θ), the initial line being the downward vertical. A small ring of mass m can slide on the wire and is attached to the point r = 0 of the cardioid by an elastic string of natural length a and modulus of elasticity 4mg. The string is released from rest when the string is horizontal. Show by using the laws of conservation of energy that
aθ̇^{2}(1 + cos θ) − g cos θ (1 − cos θ) = 0, g being the acceleration due to gravity.

**(d) [10 marks]**

For what values of the constants a, b and c the vector V̄ = (x + y + az)î + (bx + 2y − z)ĵ + (−x + cy + 2z)k̂ is irrotational. Find the divergence in cylindrical coordinates of this vector with these values.

**(e) [10 marks]**

The position vector of a moving point at time t is r̄ = sin t î + cos 2t ĵ + (t^{2} + 2t)k̂. Find the components of acceleration ā in the directions parallel to the velocity vector v̄ and perpendicular to the plane of r̄ and v̄ at time t = 0.

### Question 6

**(a) [16 marks]**

(i) Solve the following simultaneous linear differential equations:
(D + 1)y = z + e^{x} and (D + 1)z = y + e^{x} where y and z are functions of independent variable x and D ≡ d/dx. (8 marks)
(ii) If the growth rate of the population of bacteria at any time t is proportional to the amount present at that time and population doubles in one week, then how much bacterias can be expected after 4 weeks? (8 marks)

**(b) [17 marks]**

(i) Consider the differential equation xyp^{2} − (x^{2} + y^{2} − 1)p + xy = 0 where p = dy/dx. Substituting u = x^{2} and v = y^{2} reduce the equation to Clairaut's form in terms of u, v and p′ = dv/du. Hence, or otherwise solve the equation. (10 marks)
(ii) Solve the following initial value differential equations:
20y″ + 4y′ + y = 0, y(0) = 3·2 and y′(0) = 0. (7 marks)

**(c) [17 marks]**

A uniform solid hemisphere rests on a rough plane inclined to the horizon at an angle φ with its curved surface touching the plane. Find the greatest admissible value of the inclination φ for equilibrium. If φ be less than this value, is the equilibrium stable?

### Question 7

**(a) [16 marks]**

Find the curvature vector and its magnitude at any point r̄ = (θ) of the curve r̄ = (a cos θ, a sin θ, aθ). Show that the locus of the feet of the perpendicular from the origin to the tangent is a curve that completely lies on the hyperboloid x^{2} + y^{2} − z^{2} = a^{2}.

**(b) [17 marks]**

(i) Solve the differential equation:
x d^{2}y/dx^{2} − dy/dx − 4x^{3}y = 8x^{3} sin(x^{2}). (9 marks)
(ii) Solve the following differential equation using method of variation of parameters:
d^{2}y/dx^{2} − dy/dx − 2y = 44 − 76x − 48x^{2}. (8 marks)

**(c) [17 marks]**

A particle is free to move on a smooth vertical circular wire of radius a. At time t = 0 it is projected along the circle from its lowest point A with velocity just sufficient to carry it to the highest point B. Find the time T at which the reaction between the particle and the wire is zero.

### Question 8

**(a) [16 marks]**

A spherical shot of W gm weight and radius r cm, lies at the bottom of cylindrical bucket of radius R cm. The bucket is filled with water up to a depth of h cm (h > 2r). Show that the minimum amount of work done in lifting the shot just clear of the water must be [W(h − 4r^{3}/(3R^{2})) + W′(r − h + 2r^{3}/(3R^{2}))] cm gm. W′ gm is the weight of water displaced by the shot.

**(b) [17 marks]**

Solve the following initial value problem using Laplace transform:
d^{2}y/dx^{2} + 9y = r(x), y(0) = 0, y′(0) = 4
where r(x) = 8 sin x if 0 < x < π; r(x) = 0 if x ≥ π.

**(c) [17 marks]**

(i) Evaluate the integral: ∬_{S} F̄ · n̂ ds where F̄ = 3xy^{2} î + (yx^{2} − y^{3})ĵ + 3zx^{2} k̂ and S is a surface of the cylinder y^{2} + z^{2} ≤ 4, −3 ≤ x ≤ 3, using divergence theorem. (9 marks)
(ii) Using Green's theorem, evaluate the ∫_{C} F(r̄) · dr̄ counterclockwise where F(r̄) = (x^{2} + y^{2})î + (x^{2} − y^{2})ĵ and dr̄ = dx î + dy ĵ and the curve C is the boundary of the region R = {(x, y) | 1 ≤ y ≤ 2 − x^{2}}. (8 marks)

## Paper II

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let x_{1} = 2 and x_{n+1} = √(x_{n} + 20), n = 1, 2, 3, .... Show that the sequence x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, ... is convergent.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Let G be a group of order n. Show that G is isomorphic to a subgroup of the permutation group S_{n}.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Find the supremum and the infimum of x/sin x on the interval (0, π/2].

**(d) [10 marks]**

Determine all entire functions f(z) such that 0 is a removable singularity of f(1/z).

**(e) [10 marks]**

Using graphical method, find the maximum value of
2x + y
subject to
4x + 3y ≤ 12
4x + y ≤ 8
4x − y ≤ 8
x, y ≥ 0.

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Let f(t) = ∫_{0}^{t} [x] dx, where [x] denotes the largest integer less than or equal to x.
(i) Determine all the real numbers t at which f is differentiable.
(ii) Determine all the real numbers t at which f is continuous but not differentiable.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Using contour integral method, prove that
∫_{0}^{∞} x sin mx/(a^{2} + x^{2}) dx = (π/2) e^{−ma}.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Let F be a field and F[X] denote the ring of polynomials over F in a single variable X. For f(X), g(X) ∈ F[X] with g(X) ≠ 0, show that there exist q(X), r(X) ∈ F[X] such that degree (r(X)) < degree (g(X)) and
f(X) = q(X) . g(X) + r(X).

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Show that the groups ℤ_{5} × ℤ_{7} and ℤ_{35} are isomorphic.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Let f = u + iv be an analytic function on the unit disc D = {z ∈ ℂ : |z| < 1}. Show that
∂^{2}u/∂x^{2} + ∂^{2}u/∂y^{2} = 0 = ∂^{2}v/∂x^{2} + ∂^{2}v/∂y^{2}
at all points of D.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Solve the following linear programming problem by simplex method:
Maximize
z = 3x_{1} + 5x_{2} + 4x_{3}
subject to
2x_{1} + 3x_{2} ≤ 8
2x_{2} + 5x_{3} ≤ 10
3x_{1} + 2x_{2} + 4x_{3} ≤ 15
x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3} ≥ 0.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

For a function f : ℂ → ℂ and n ≥ 1, let f^{(n)} denote the n^{th} derivative of f and f^{(0)} = f. Let f be an entire function such that for some n ≥ 1, f^{(n)}(1/k) = 0 for all k = 1, 2, 3, .... Show that f is a polynomial.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the initial basic feasible solution of the following transportation problem using Vogel's approximation method and find the cost.

| Origin | D_{1} | D_{2} | D_{3} | D_{4} | D_{5} | Supply |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| O_{1} | 4 | 7 | 0 | 3 | 6 | 14 |
| O_{2} | 1 | 2 | −3 | 3 | 8 | 9 |
| O_{3} | 3 | −1 | 4 | 0 | 5 | 17 |
| Demand | 8 | 3 | 8 | 13 | 8 | |

**(c) [20 marks]**

Let ∑_{n=1}^{∞} x_{n} be a conditionally convergent series of real numbers. Show that there is a rearrangement ∑_{n=1}^{∞} x_{π(n)} of the series ∑_{n=1}^{∞} x_{n} that converges to 100.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Solve (D^{2} − 2DD′ + D′^{2})z = e^{x+2y} + x^{3} + sin 2x,
where D ≡ ∂/∂x, D′ ≡ ∂/∂y, D^{2} ≡ ∂^{2}/∂x^{2}, D′^{2} ≡ ∂^{2}/∂y^{2}.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Explain the main steps of the Gauss-Jordan method and apply this method to find the inverse of the matrix
[[2, 6, 6], [2, 8, 6], [2, 6, 8]].

**(c) [10 marks]**

Write the Boolean expression
z(y + z)(x + y + z)
in its simplest form using Boolean postulate rules. Mention the rules used during simplification. Verify your result by constructing the truth table for the given expression and for its simplest form.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Let Γ be a closed curve in xy-plane and let S denote the region bounded by the curve Γ. Let
∂^{2}w/∂x^{2} + ∂^{2}w/∂y^{2} = f(x, y) ∀ (x, y) ∈ S.
If f is prescribed at each point (x, y) of S and w is prescribed on the boundary Γ of S, then prove that any solution w = w(x, y), satisfying these conditions, is unique.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Show that the moment of inertia of an elliptic area of mass M and semi-axis a and b about a semi-diameter of length r is (1/4)M a^{2}b^{2}/r^{2}. Further, prove that the moment of inertia about a tangent is (5M/4)p^{2}, where p is the perpendicular distance from the centre of the ellipse to the tangent.

### Question 6

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find a complete integral of the partial differential equation
2(pq + yp + qx) + x^{2} + y^{2} = 0.

**(b) [15 marks]**

For given equidistant values u_{−1}, u_{0}, u_{1} and u_{2}, a value is interpolated by Lagrange's formula. Show that it may be written in the form
u_{x} = yu_{0} + xu_{1} + (y(y^{2} − 1)/3!) Δ^{2}u_{−1} + (x(x^{2} − 1)/3!) Δ^{2}u_{0},
where x + y = 1.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Two uniform rods AB, AC, each of mass m and length 2a, are smoothly hinged together at A and move on a horizontal plane. At time t, the mass centre of the rods is at the point (ξ, η) referred to fixed perpendicular axes Ox, Oy in the plane, and the rods make angles θ ± φ with Ox. Prove that the kinetic energy of the system is
m[ξ̇^{2} + η̇^{2} + (1/3 + sin^{2}φ)a^{2}θ̇^{2} + (1/3 + cos^{2}φ)a^{2}φ̇^{2}].
Also derive Lagrange's equations of motion for the system if an external force with components [X, Y] along the axes acts at A.

### Question 7

**(a) [15 marks]**

Reduce the equation
y^{2} ∂^{2}z/∂x^{2} − 2xy ∂^{2}z/∂x∂y + x^{2} ∂^{2}z/∂y^{2} = (y^{2}/x) ∂z/∂x + (x^{2}/y) ∂z/∂y
to canonical form and hence solve it.

**(b) [20 marks]**

Derive the formula
∫_{a}^{b} y dx = (3h/8)[(y_{0} + y_{n}) + 3(y_{1} + y_{2} + y_{4} + y_{5} + ... + y_{n−1}) + 2(y_{3} + y_{6} + ... + y_{n−3})].
Is there any restriction on n? State that condition. What is the error bound in the case of Simpson's 3/8 rule?

**(c) [15 marks]**

A stream is rushing from a boiler through a conical pipe, the diameters of the ends of which are D and d. If V and v be the corresponding velocities of the stream and if the motion is assumed to be steady and diverging from the vertex of the cone, then prove that
v/V = (D^{2}/d^{2}) e^{(v^{2} − V^{2})/2K},
where K is the pressure divided by the density and is constant.

### Question 8

**(a) [20 marks]**

Given the one-dimensional wave equation
∂^{2}y/∂t^{2} = c^{2} ∂^{2}y/∂x^{2}; t > 0,
where c^{2} = T/m, T is the constant tension in the string and m is the mass per unit length of the string.
(i) Find the appropriate solution of the above wave equation.
(ii) Find also the solution under the conditions
y(0, t) = 0, y(l, t) = 0 for all t
and [∂y/∂t]_{t=0} = 0, y(x, 0) = a sin(πx/l), 0 < x < l, a > 0.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Write an algorithm in the form of a flow chart for Newton-Raphson method. Describe the cases of failure of this method.

**(c) [15 marks]**

If the velocity of an incompressible fluid at the point (x, y, z) is given by
(3xz/r^{5}, 3yz/r^{5}, (3z^{2} − r^{2})/r^{5}), r^{2} = x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2},
then prove that the liquid motion is possible and that the velocity potential is z/r^{3}. Further, determine the streamlines.

## Paper I

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let f : [0, π/2] → ℝ be a continuous function such that
f(x) = cos^{2}x/(4x^{2} − π^{2}), 0 ≤ x < π/2.
Find the value of f(π/2).

**(b) [10 marks]**

Let f : D (⊆ ℝ^{2}) → ℝ be a function and (a, b) ∈ D. If f(x, y) is continuous at (a, b), then show that the functions f(x, b) and f(a, y) are continuous at x = a and at y = b respectively.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Let T : ℝ^{2} → ℝ^{2} be a linear map such that T(2, 1) = (5, 7) and T(1, 2) = (3, 3). If A is the matrix corresponding to T with respect to the standard bases e_{1}, e_{2}, then find Rank (A).

**(d) [10 marks]**

If
A = [[1, 2, 1], [1, −4, 1], [3, 0, −3]] and B = [[2, 1, 1], [1, −1, 0], [2, 1, −1]]
then show that AB = 6I_{3}. Use this result to solve the following system of equations :
2x + y + z = 5
x − y = 0
2x + y − z = 1

**(e) [10 marks]**

Show that the lines
(x + 1)/(−3) = (y − 3)/2 = (z + 2)/1 and x/1 = (y − 7)/(−3) = (z + 7)/2
intersect. Find the coordinates of the point of intersection and the equation of the plane containing them.

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Is f(x) = |cos x| + |sin x| differentiable at x = π/2 ? If yes, then find its derivative at x = π/2. If no, then give a proof of it.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Let A and B be two orthogonal matrices of same order and det A + det B = 0. Show that A + B is a singular matrix.

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) The plane x + 2y + 3z = 12 cuts the axes of coordinates in A, B, C. Find the equations of the circle circumscribing the triangle ABC. (10 marks)
(ii) Prove that the plane z = 0 cuts the enveloping cone of the sphere x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} = 11 which has the vertex at (2, 4, 1) in a rectangular hyperbola. (10 marks)

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the maximum and the minimum value of the function f(x) = 2x^{3} − 9x^{2} + 12x + 6 on the interval [2, 3].

**(b) [15 marks]**

Prove that, in general, three normals can be drawn from a given point to the paraboloid x^{2} + y^{2} = 2az, but if the point lies on the surface
27a(x^{2} + y^{2}) + 8(a − z)^{3} = 0
then two of the three normals coincide.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Let
A = [[5, 7, 2, 1], [1, 1, −8, 1], [2, 3, 5, 0], [3, 4, −3, 1]]
(i) Find the rank of matrix A.
(ii) Find the dimension of the subspace
V = {(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}) ∈ ℝ^{4} | A[[x_{1}], [x_{2}], [x_{3}], [x_{4}]] = 0}
(15 + 5 = 20 marks)

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

State the Cayley-Hamilton theorem. Use this theorem to find A^{100}, where
A = [[1, 0, 0], [1, 0, 1], [0, 1, 0]]

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the length of the normal chord through a point P of the ellipsoid
x^{2}/a^{2} + y^{2}/b^{2} + z^{2}/c^{2} = 1
and prove that if it is equal to 4PG_{3}, where G_{3} is the point where the normal chord through P meets the xy-plane, then P lies on the cone
(x^{2}/a^{6})(2c^{2} − a^{2}) + (y^{2}/b^{6})(2c^{2} − b^{2}) + z^{2}/c^{4} = 0

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) If
u = sin^{−1} √((x^{1/3} + y^{1/3})/(x^{1/2} + y^{1/2}))
then show that sin^{2}u is a homogeneous function of x and y of degree −1/6. Hence show that
x^{2} ∂^{2}u/∂x^{2} + 2xy ∂^{2}u/∂x∂y + y^{2} ∂^{2}u/∂y^{2} = (tan u/12)(13/12 + tan^{2}u/12) (12 marks)
(ii) Using the Jacobian method, show that if f′(x) = 1/(1 + x^{2}) and f(0) = 0, then
f(x) + f(y) = f((x + y)/(1 − xy)) (8 marks)

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Solve the differential equation
(2y sin x + 3y^{4} sin x cos x) dx − (4y^{3} cos^{2}x + cos x) dy = 0

**(b) [10 marks]**

Determine the complete solution of the differential equation
d^{2}y/dx^{2} − 4 dy/dx + 4y = 3x^{2}e^{2x} sin 2x

**(c) [10 marks]**

One end of a heavy uniform rod AB can slide along a rough horizontal rod AC, to which it is attached by a ring. B and C are joined by a string. When the rod is on the point of sliding, then AC^{2} − AB^{2} = BC^{2}. If θ is the angle between AB and the horizontal line, then prove that the coefficient of friction is cot θ/(2 + cot^{2}θ).

**(d) [10 marks]**

The force of attraction of a particle by the earth is inversely proportional to the square of its distance from the earth's centre. A particle, whose weight on the surface of the earth is W, falls to the surface of the earth from a height 3h above it. Show that the magnitude of work done by the earth's attraction force is (3/4)hW, where h is the radius of the earth.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Find the directional derivative of the function xy^{2} + yz^{2} + zx^{2} along the tangent to the curve x = t, y = t^{2}, z = t^{3} at the point (1, 1, 1).

### Question 6

**(a) [15 marks]**

A body consists of a cone and underlying hemisphere. The base of the cone and the top of the hemisphere have same radius a. The whole body rests on a rough horizontal table with hemisphere in contact with the table. Show that the greatest height of the cone, so that the equilibrium may be stable, is √3 a.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the circulation of **F** round the curve C, where **F** = (2x + y^{2})**i** + (3y − 4x)**j** and C is the curve y = x^{2} from (0, 0) to (1, 1) and the curve y^{2} = x from (1, 1) to (0, 0).

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Solve the differential equation
d^{2}y/dx^{2} + (3 sin x − cot x) dy/dx + 2y sin^{2}x = e^{−cos x} sin^{2}x (10 marks)
(ii) Find the Laplace transforms of t^{−1/2} and t^{1/2}. Prove that the Laplace transform of t^{n + 1/2}, where n ∈ ℕ, is
Γ(n + 1 + 1/2)/s^{n + 1 + 1/2} (10 marks)

### Question 7

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the linearly independent solutions of the corresponding homogeneous differential equation of the equation x^{2}y″ − 2xy′ + 2y = x^{3} sin x and then find the general solution of the given equation by the method of variation of parameters.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the radius of curvature and radius of torsion of the helix x = a cos u, y = a sin u, z = au tan α.

**(c) [20 marks]**

A particle moving along the y-axis has an acceleration Fy towards the origin, where F is a positive and even function of y. The periodic time, when the particle vibrates between y = −a and y = a, is T. Show that
2π/√F_{1} < T < 2π/√F_{2}
where F_{1} and F_{2} are the greatest and the least values of F within the range [−a, a]. Further, show that when a simple pendulum of length l oscillates through 30° on either side of the vertical line, T lies between 2π√(l/g) and 2π√(l/g) √(π/3).

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Obtain the singular solution of the differential equation
(dy/dx)^{2} (y/x)^{2} cot^{2}α − 2(dy/dx)(y/x) + (y/x)^{2} cosec^{2}α = 1
Also find the complete primitive of the given differential equation. Give the geometrical interpretations of the complete primitive and singular solution.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Prove that the path of a planet, which is moving so that its acceleration is always directed to a fixed point (star) and is equal to μ/(distance)^{2}, is a conic section. Find the conditions under which the path becomes (i) ellipse, (ii) parabola and (iii) hyperbola.

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) State Gauss divergence theorem. Verify this theorem for **F** = 4x**i** − 2y^{2}**j** + z^{2}**k**, taken over the region bounded by x^{2} + y^{2} = 4, z = 0 and z = 3. (15 marks)
(ii) Evaluate by Stokes' theorem ∮_{C} e^{x} dx + 2y dy − dz, where C is the curve x^{2} + y^{2} = 4, z = 2. (5 marks)

## Paper II

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let G be a finite group, H and K subgroups of G such that K ⊂ H. Show that (G : K) = (G : H)(H : K).

**(b) [10 marks]**

Show that the function
f(x, y) = (x^{2} − y^{2})/(x − y), (x, y) ≠ (1, −1), (1, 1)
= 0, (x, y) = (1, 1), (1, −1)
is continuous and differentiable at (1, −1).

**(c) [10 marks]**

Evaluate
∫_{0}^{∞} tan^{−1}(ax)/(x(1 + x^{2})) dx, a > 0, a ≠ 1.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Suppose f(z) is analytic function on a domain D in ℂ and satisfies the equation Im f(z) = (Re f(z))^{2}, z ∈ D. Show that f(z) is constant in D.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Use graphical method to solve the linear programming problem.
Maximize Z = 3x_{1} + 2x_{2}
subject to
x_{1} − x_{2} ≥ 1,
x_{1} + x_{3} ≥ 3
and x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3} ≥ 0

### Question 2

**(a) [10 marks]**

If G and H are finite groups whose orders are relatively prime, then prove that there is only one homomorphism from G to H, the trivial one.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Write down all quotient groups of the group Z_{12}.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Using differentials, find an approximate value of f(4.1, 4.9) where
f(x, y) = (x^{3} + x^{2}y)^{1/2}.

**(d) [15 marks]**

Show that an isolated singular point z_{0} of a function f(z) is a pole of order m if and only if f(z) can be written in the form f(z) = φ(z)/(z − z_{0})^{m} where φ(z) is analytic and non zero at z_{0}.
Moreover Res_{z = z_{0}} f(z) = φ^{(m−1)}(z_{0})/(m − 1)! if m ≥ 1.

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Discuss the uniform convergence of
f_{n}(x) = nx/(1 + n^{2}x^{2}), ∀ x ∈ ℝ (−∞, ∞)
n = 1, 2, 3, ....

**(b) [15 marks]**

Solve the linear programming problem using Simplex method.
Minimize Z = x_{1} + 2x_{2} − 3x_{3} − 2x_{4}
subject to
x_{1} + 2x_{2} − 3x_{3} + x_{4} = 4
x_{1} + 2x_{2} + x_{3} + 2x_{4} = 4
and x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4} ≥ 0

**(c) [10 marks]**

Evaluate the integral ∫_{C} Re(z^{2}) dz from 0 to 2 + 4i along the curve C where C is a parabola y = x^{2}.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Let a be an irreducible element of the Euclidean ring R, then prove that R/(a) is a field.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the maximum value of f(x, y, z) = x^{2}y^{2}z^{2} subject to the subsidiary condition x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} = c^{2}, (x, y, z > 0).

**(b) [10 marks]**

Obtain the first three terms of the Laurent series expansion of the function f(z) = 1/(e^{z} − 1) about the point z = 0 valid in the region 0 < |z| < 2π.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Discuss the convergence of ∫_{1}^{2} √x/ln x dx.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Consider the following LPP,
Maximize Z = 2x_{1} + 4x_{2} + 4x_{3} − 3x_{4}
subject to
x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3} = 4
x_{1} + 4x_{2} + x_{4} = 8
and x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4} ≥ 0
Use the dual problem to verify that the basic solution (x_{1}, x_{2}) is not optimal.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Form a partial differential equation of the family of surfaces given by the following expression :
ψ(x^{2} + y^{2} + 2z^{2}, y^{2} − 2zx) = 0.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Apply Newton-Raphson method, to find a real root of transcendental equation x log_{10}x = 1.2, correct to three decimal places.

**(c) [10 marks]**

A uniform rod OA, of length 2a, free to turn about its end O, revolves with angular velocity ω about the vertical OZ through O, and is inclined at a constant angle α to OZ; find the value of α.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Using Runge-Kutta method of fourth order, solve dy/dx = (y^{2} − x^{2})/(y^{2} + x^{2}) with y(0) = 1 at x = 0.2. Use four decimal places for calculation and step length 0.2.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Draw a flow chart and write a basic algorithm (in FORTRAN/C/C^{++}) for evaluating y = ∫_{0}^{6} dx/(1 + x^{2}) using Trapezoidal rule.

### Question 6

**(a) [15 marks]**

Solve the first order quasilinear partial differential equation by the method of characteristics :
x ∂u/∂x + (u − x − y) ∂u/∂y = x + 2y in x > 0, −∞ < y < ∞ with u = 1 + y on x = 1.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the equivalent numbers given in a specified number to the system mentioned against them :
(i) Integer 524 in binary system.
(ii) 101010110101.101101011 to octal system.
(iii) decimal number 5280 to hexadecimal system.
(iv) Find the unknown number (1101.101)_{8} → (?)_{10}.

**(c) [20 marks]**

A circular cylinder of radius a and radius of gyration k rolls without slipping inside a fixed hollow cylinder of radius b. Show that the plane through axes moves in a circular pendulum of length (b − a)(1 + k^{2}/a^{2}).

### Question 7

**(b) [15 marks]**

∂^{2}u/∂x^{2} − 2x ∂^{2}u/∂x∂y + x^{2} ∂^{2}u/∂y^{2} = ∂u/∂y + 12x.

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Given the Boolean expression
X = AB + ABC + AB̄C̄ + AC̄
(i) Draw the logical diagram for the expression.
(ii) Minimize the expression.
(iii) Draw the logical diagram for the reduced expression.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Π + (1/2)ρ{d^{2}R^{2}/dt^{2} + (dR/dt)^{2}}.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Two sources, each of strength m, are placed at the points (−a, 0), (a, 0) and a sink of strength 2m at origin. Show that the stream lines are the curves (x^{2} + y^{2})^{2} = a^{2}(x^{2} − y^{2} + λxy), where λ is a variable parameter.
Show also that the fluid speed at any point is (2ma^{2})/(r_{1}r_{2}r_{3}), where r_{1}, r_{2} and r_{3} are the distances of the points from the sources and the sink, respectively.

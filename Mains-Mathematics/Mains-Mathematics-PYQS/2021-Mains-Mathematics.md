## Paper I

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

If A = [[1, −1, 1], [2, −1, 0], [1, 0, 0]], then show that A^{2} = A^{−1} (without finding A^{−1}).

**(b) [10 marks]**

Find the matrix associated with the linear operator on V_{3}(R) defined by T(a, b, c) = (a + b, a − b, 2c) with respect to the ordered basis B = {(0, 1, 1), (1, 0, 1), (1, 1, 0)}.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Given : Δ(x) = |[f(x + α), f(x + 2α), f(x + 3α)], [f(α), f(2α), f(3α)], [f′(α), f′(2α), f′(3α)]| where f is a real valued differentiable function and α is a constant. Find lim_{x→0} Δ(x)/x.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Show that between any two roots of e^{x} cos x = 1, there exists at least one root of e^{x} sin x − 1 = 0.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Find the equation of the cylinder whose generators are parallel to the line x = −y/2 = z/3 and whose guiding curve is x^{2} + 2y^{2} = 1, z = 0.

### Question 2

**(a) [20 marks]**

Show that the planes, which cut the cone ax^{2} + by^{2} + cz^{2} = 0 in perpendicular generators, touch the cone x^{2}/(b + c) + y^{2}/(c + a) + z^{2}/(a + b) = 0.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Given that f(x, y) = |x^{2} − y^{2}|. Find f_{xy}(0, 0) and f_{yx}(0, 0). Hence show that f_{xy}(0, 0) = f_{yx}(0, 0).

**(c) [15 marks]**

Show that S = {(x, 2y, 3x) : x, y are real numbers} is a subspace of R^{3}(R). Find two bases of S. Also find the dimension of S.

### Question 3

**(a) [20 marks]**

(i) If u = x^{2} + y^{2}, v = x^{2} − y^{2}, where x = r cos θ, y = r sin θ, then find ∂(u, v)/∂(r, θ). (7 marks)
(ii) If ∫_{0}^{x} f(t) dt = x + ∫_{x}^{1} t f(t) dt, then find the value of f(1). (5 marks)
(iii) Express ∫_{a}^{b} (x − a)^{m}(b − x)^{n} dx in terms of Beta function. (8 marks)

**(b) [15 marks]**

A sphere of constant radius r passes through the origin O and cuts the axes at the points A, B and C. Find, the locus of the foot of the perpendicular drawn from O to the plane ABC.

**(c) [15 marks]**

(i) Prove that the eigen vectors, corresponding to two distinct eigen values of a real symmetric matrix, are orthogonal. (8 marks)
(ii) For two square matrices A and B of order 2, show that trace (AB) = trace (BA). Hence show that AB − BA ≠ I_{2}, where I_{2} is an identity matrix of order 2. (7 marks)

### Question 4

**(a) [20 marks]**

(i) Reduce the following matrix to a row-reduced echelon form and hence also, find its rank : A = [[1, 3, 2, 4, 1], [0, 0, 2, 2, 0], [2, 6, 2, 6, 2], [3, 9, 1, 10, 6]] (10 marks)
(ii) Find the eigen values and the corresponding eigen vectors of the matrix A = [[0, −i], [i, 0]], over the complex-number field. (10 marks)

**(b) [15 marks]**

Show that the entire area of the Astroid : x^{2/3} + y^{2/3} = a^{2/3} is (3/8)πa^{2}.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Find equation of the plane containing the lines (x + 1)/3 = (y + 3)/5 = (z + 5)/7, (x − 2)/1 = (y − 4)/3 = (z − 6)/5. Also find the point of intersection of the given lines.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Solve the differential equation : d^{2}y/dx^{2} + 2y = x^{2}e^{3x} + e^{x} cos 2x

**(b) [10 marks]**

Solve the initial value problem : d^{2}y/dx^{2} + 4y = e^{−2x} sin 2x; y(0) = y′(0) = 0 using Laplace transform method.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Two rods LM and MN are joined rigidly at the point M such that (LM)^{2} + (MN)^{2} = (LN)^{2} and they are hanged freely in equilibrium from a fixed point L. Let ω be the weight per unit length of both the rods which are uniform. Determine the angle, which the rod LM makes with the vertical direction, in terms of lengths of the rods.

**(d) [10 marks]**

If a planet, which revolves around the Sun in a circular orbit, is suddenly stopped in its orbit, then find the time in which it would fall into the Sun. Also, find the ratio of its falling time to the period of revolution of the planet.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Show that ∇^{2}[∇ · (**r**/r)] = 2/r^{4}, where **r** = xî + yĵ + zk̂.

### Question 6

**(a) [20 marks]**

A heavy string, which is not of uniform density, is hung up from two points. Let T_{1}, T_{2}, T_{3} be the tensions at the intermediate points A, B, C of the catenary respectively where its inclinations to the horizontal are in arithmetic progression with common difference β. Let ω_{1} and ω_{2} be the weights of the parts AB and BC of the string respectively. Prove that
(i) Harmonic mean of T_{1}, T_{2} and T_{3} = 3T_{2}/(1 + 2 cos β)
(ii) T_{1}/T_{3} = ω_{1}/ω_{2}

**(b) [15 marks]**

Solve the equation : d^{2}y/dx^{2} + (tan x − 3 cos x) dy/dx + 2y cos^{2}x = cos^{4}x completely by demonstrating all the steps involved.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Evaluate ∫_{C} **F** · d**r**, where C is an arbitrary closed curve in the xy-plane and **F** = (−yî + xĵ)/(x^{2} + y^{2}).

### Question 7

**(a) [20 marks]**

Verify Gauss divergence theorem for **F** = 2x^{2}yî − y^{2}ĵ + 4xz^{2}k̂ taken over the region in the first octant bounded by y^{2} + z^{2} = 9 and x = 2.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find all possible solutions of the differential equation : y^{2} log y = xy dy/dx + (dy/dx)^{2}.

**(c) [15 marks]**

A heavy particle hangs by an inextensible string of length a from a fixed point and is then projected horizontally with a velocity √(2gh). If 5a/2 > h > a, then prove that the circular motion ceases when the particle has reached the height (1/3)(a + 2h) from the point of projection. Also, prove that the greatest height ever reached by the particle above the point of projection is (4a − h)(a + 2h)^{2}/(27a^{2}).

### Question 8

**(a) [20 marks]**

(i) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of confocal conics x^{2}/(a^{2} + λ) + y^{2}/(b^{2} + λ) = 1; a > b > 0 are constants and λ is a parameter. Show that the given family of curves is self orthogonal. (10 marks)
(ii) Find the general solution of the differential equation : x^{2} d^{2}y/dx^{2} − 2x(1 + x) dy/dx + 2(1 + x)y = 0. Hence, solve the differential equation : x^{2} d^{2}y/dx^{2} − 2x(1 + x) dy/dx + 2(1 + x)y = x^{3} by the method of variation of parameters. (10 marks)

**(b) [15 marks]**

Describe the motion and path of a particle of mass m which is projected in a vertical plane through a point of projection with velocity u in a direction making an angle θ with the horizontal direction. Further, if particles are projected from that point in the same vertical plane with velocity 4√g, then determine the locus of vertices of their paths.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Using Stokes’ theorem, evaluate ∬_{S} (∇ × **F**) · n̂ dS, where **F** = (x^{2} + y − 4)î + 3xyĵ + (2xy + z^{2})k̂ and S is the surface of the paraboloid z = 4 − (x^{2} + y^{2}) above the xy-plane. Here, n̂ is the unit outward normal vector on S.

## Paper II

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let m_{1}, m_{2}, ⋯, m_{k} be positive integers and d > 0 the greatest common divisor of m_{1}, m_{2}, ⋯, m_{k}. Show that there exist integers x_{1}, x_{2}, ⋯, x_{k} such that d = x_{1}m_{1} + x_{2}m_{2} + ⋯ + x_{k}m_{k}

**(b) [10 marks]**

Test the uniform convergence of the series x^{4} + x^{4}/(1 + x^{4}) + x^{4}/(1 + x^{4})^{2} + x^{4}/(1 + x^{4})^{3} + ⋯ on [0, 1].

**(c) [10 marks]**

If a function f is monotonic in the interval [a, b], then prove that f is Riemann integrable in [a, b].

**(d) [10 marks]**

Let c : [0, 1] → ℂ be the curve, where c(t) = e^{4πit}, 0 ≤ t ≤ 1. Evaluate the contour integral ∫_{c} dz/(2z^{2} − 5z + 2).

**(e) [10 marks]**

A department of a company has five employees with five jobs to be performed. The time (in hours) that each man takes to perform each job is given in the effectiveness matrix. Assign all the jobs to these five employees to minimize the total processing time :

| Job | I | II | III | IV | V |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 10 | 5 | 13 | 15 | 16 |
| B | 3 | 9 | 18 | 13 | 6 |
| C | 10 | 7 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| D | 7 | 11 | 9 | 7 | 12 |
| E | 7 | 9 | 10 | 4 | 12 |

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the maximum and minimum values of f(x) = x^{3} − 9x^{2} + 26x − 24 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 1.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Let F be a field and f(x) ∈ F[x] a polynomial of degree > 0 over F. Show that there is a field F′ and an imbedding q : F → F′ s.t. the polynomial f^{q} ∈ F′[x] has a root in F′, where f^{q} is obtained by replacing each coefficient a of f by q(a).

**(c) [20 marks]**

Find the Laurent series expansion of f(z) = (z^{2} − z + 1)/(z(z^{2} − 3z + 2)) in the powers of (z + 1) in the region |z + 1| > 3.

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Let f be an entire function whose Taylor series expansion with centre z = 0 has infinitely many terms. Show that z = 0 is an essential singularity of f(1/z).

**(b) [20 marks]**

Find the stationary values of x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} subject to the conditions ax^{2} + by^{2} + cz^{2} = 1 and lx + my + nz = 0. Interpret the result geometrically.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Convert the following LPP into dual LPP :
Minimize Z = x_{1} − 3x_{2} − 2x_{3}
subject to 3x_{1} − x_{2} + 2x_{3} ≤ 7
2x_{1} − 4x_{2} ≥ 12
−4x_{1} + 3x_{2} + 8x_{3} = 10
where x_{1}, x_{2} ≥ 0 and x_{3} is unrestricted in sign.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Show that there are infinitely many subgroups of the additive group ℚ of rational numbers.

**(b) [20 marks]**

Using contour integration, evaluate the integral ∫_{−∞}^{∞} sin x dx/(x(x^{2} + a^{2})), a > 0.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Solve the following linear programming problem using Big M method :
Maximize Z = 4x_{1} + 5x_{2} + 2x_{3}
subject to 2x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3} ≥ 10
x_{1} + 3x_{2} + x_{3} ≤ 12
x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3} = 6
x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3} ≥ 0

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Obtain the partial differential equation by eliminating arbitrary function f from the equation f(x + y + z, x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2}) = 0.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Find a positive root of the equation 3x = 1 + cos x by a numerical technique using initial values 0, π/2; and further improve the result using Newton-Raphson method correct to 8 significant figures.

**(c) [10 marks]**

(i) Convert (3798·3875)_{10} into octal and hexadecimal equivalents.
(ii) Obtain the principal conjunctive normal form of (¬P → R) ∧ (Q ⇄ P).

**(d) [10 marks]**

A particle is constrained to move along a circle lying in the vertical xy-plane. With the help of the D’Alembert’s principle, show that its equation of motion is ẍy − ÿx − gx = 0, where g is the acceleration due to gravity.

**(e) [10 marks]**

What arrangements of sources and sinks can have the velocity potential w = log_{e}(z − a^{2}/z)? Draw the corresponding sketch of the streamlines and prove that two of them subdivide into the circle r = a and the axis of y.

### Question 6

Solve the wave equation a^{2} ∂^{2}u/∂x^{2} = ∂^{2}u/∂t^{2}, 0 < x < L, t > 0 subject to the conditions u(0, t) = 0, u(L, t) = 0, u(x, 0) = (1/4)x(L − x), ∂u/∂t|_{t=0} = 0

**(b) [15 marks]**

Obtain the Boolean function F(x, y, z) based on the table given below. Then simplify F(x, y, z) and draw the corresponding GATE network :

**(c) [15 marks]**

Obtain the Lagrangian equation for the motion of a system of two particles of unequal masses connected by an inextensible string passing over a small smooth pulley.

### Question 7

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the general solution of the partial differential equation (D^{2} − D′^{2} − 3D + 3D′)z = xy + e^{x+2y} where D ≡ ∂/∂x and D′ ≡ ∂/∂y.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Solve the system of equations 3x_{1} + 9x_{2} − 2x_{3} = 11, 4x_{1} + 2x_{2} + 13x_{3} = 24, 4x_{1} − 2x_{2} + x_{3} = −8 correct up to 4 significant figures by using Gauss-Seidel method after verifying whether the method is applicable in your transformed form of the system.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Show that **q** = λ(−yî + xĵ)/(x^{2} + y^{2}), (λ = constant) is a possible incompressible fluid motion. Determine the streamlines. Is the kind of the motion potential? If yes, then find the velocity potential.

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find a complete integral of the partial differential equation p = (z + qy)^{2} by using Charpit’s method.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Derive Newton’s backward difference interpolation formula and also do error analysis.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Show that for the complex potential tan^{−1}z, the streamlines and equipotential curves are circles. Find the velocity at any point and check the singularities at z = ±i.

## Paper I

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let V_{1} = (2, −1, 3, 2), V_{2} = (−1, 1, 1, −3) and V_{3} = (1, 1, 9, −5) be three vectors of the space ℝ^{4}. Does (3, −1, 0, −1) ∈ span {V_{1}, V_{2}, V_{3}}? Justify your answer.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Find the rank and nullity of the linear transformation: T : ℝ^{3} → ℝ^{3} given by T(x, y, z) = (x + z, x + y + 2z, 2x + y + 3z)

**(c) [10 marks]**

Find the values of p and q for which lim_{x→0} [x(1 + p cos x) − q sin x]/x^{3} exists and equals 1.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Examine the convergence of the integral ∫_{0}^{1} (log x)/(1 + x) dx

**(e) [10 marks]**

A variable plane which is at a constant distance 3p from the origin O cuts the axes in the points A, B, C respectively. Show that the locus of the centroid of the tetrahedron OABC is 9(1/x^{2} + 1/y^{2} + 1/z^{2}) = 16/p^{2}.

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

If the matrix of a linear transformation T : ℝ^{3} → ℝ^{3} relative to the basis {(1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0), (0, 0, 1)} is [[1, 1, 2], [−1, 2, 1], [0, 1, 3]], then find the matrix of T relative to the basis {(1, 1, 1), (0, 1, 1), (0, 0, 1)}.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Evaluate the triple integral which gives the volume of the solid enclosed between the two paraboloids Z = 5(x^{2} + y^{2}) and Z = 6 − 7x^{2} − y^{2}.

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Show that the equation 2x^{2} + 3y^{2} − 8x + 6y − 12z + 11 = 0 represents an elliptic paraboloid. Also find its principal axis and principal planes. (10)
(ii) The plane x/a + y/b + z/c = 1 meets the coordinate axes in A, B, C respectively. Prove that the equation of the cone generated by the lines drawn from the origin O to meet the circle ABC is yz(b/c + c/b) + zx(c/a + a/c) + xy(b/a + a/b) = 0. (10)

### Question 3

**(a) [20 marks]**

Let A = [[1, 0, 0], [1, 0, 1], [0, 1, 0]]
(i) Verify the Cayley-Hamilton theorem for the matrix A.
(ii) Show that A^{n} = A^{n−2} + A^{2} − I for n ≥ 3, where I is the identity matrix of order 3. Hence, find A^{40}. (10+10)

**(b) [15 marks]**

Justify whether (0, 0) is an extreme point for the function f(x, y) = 2x^{4} − 3x^{2}y + y^{2}.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Find the equation of the sphere through the circle x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} − 4x − 6y + 2z − 16 = 0; 3x + y + 3z − 4 = 0 in the following two cases.
(i) the point (1, 0, −3) lies on the sphere.
(ii) the given circle is a great circle of the sphere.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the rank of the matrix A = [[1, 2, −1, 0], [−1, 3, 0, −4], [2, 1, 3, −2], [1, 1, 1, −1]] by reducing it to row-reduced echelon form.

**(b) [20 marks]**

Trace the curve y^{2}(x^{2} − 1) = 2x − 1.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Prove that the locus of a line which meets the lines y = mx, z = c; y = −mx, z = −c and the circle x^{2} + y^{2} = a^{2}, z = 0 is c^{2}m^{2}(cy − mzx)^{2} + c^{2}(yz − cmx)^{2} = a^{2}m^{2}(z^{2} − c^{2})^{2}.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Obtain the solution of the initial-value problem dy/dx − 2xy = 2, y(0) = 1 in the form y = e^{x^{2}}[1 + √π erf(x)].

**(b) [10 marks]**

Given that L{f(t); p} = F(p). Show that ∫_{0}^{∞} (f(t)/t) dt = ∫_{0}^{∞} F(x) dx. Hence evaluate the integral ∫_{0}^{∞} (e^{−t} − e^{−3t})/t dt.

**(c) [10 marks]**

A cylinder of radius 'a' touches a vertical wall along a generating line. Axis of the cylinder is fixed horizontally. A uniform flat beam of length '*l*' and weight 'W' rests with its extremities in contact with the wall and the cylinder, making an angle of 45° with the vertical. If frictional forces are neglected, then show that a/*l* = (√5 + 5)/(4√2). Also, find the reactions of the cylinder and wall.

**(d) [10 marks]**

A particle is moving under Simple Harmonic Motion of period T about a centre O. It passes through the point P with velocity v along the direction OP and OP = p. Find the time that elapses before the particle returns to the point P. What will be the value of p when the elapsed time is T/2?

**(e) [10 marks]**

If **a** = sin θ î + cos θ ĵ + θ k̂, **b** = cos θ î − sin θ ĵ − 3k̂, **c** = 2î + 3ĵ − 3k̂, then find the values of the derivative of the vector function **a** × (**b** × **c**) w.r.t. θ at θ = π/2 and θ = π.

### Question 6

Solve the differential equation: d^{3}y/dx^{3} − 3 d^{2}y/dx^{2} + 4 dy/dx − 2y = e^{x} + cos x.

**(b) [15 marks]**

If it is further projected from a point O_{2}, which is vertically above O_{1} at a height h in the same vertical plane, with the same velocity v and same angle θ with the horizon, its horizontal range is R_{2}. Prove that R_{2} > R_{1} and (R_{2} − R_{1}) : R_{1} is equal to (1/2){√(1 + 2gh/(v^{2} sin^{2} θ)) − 1} : 1

**(c) [20 marks]**

Evaluate the integral ∬_{S} (3y^{2}z^{2} î + 4z^{2}x^{2} ĵ + z^{2}y^{2} k̂) · n̂ dS, where S is the upper part of the surface 4x^{2} + 4y^{2} + 4z^{2} = 1 above the plane z = 0 and bounded by the xy-plane. Hence, verify Gauss-Divergence theorem.

### Question 7

**(a) [20 marks]**

(i) Find the solution of the differential equation: dy/dx = −(2xy^{3} + 2)/(3x^{2}y^{2} + 8e^{4y}) (10)
(ii) Reduce the equation x^{2}p^{2} + y(2x + y)p + y^{2} = 0 to Clairaut's form by the substitution y = u and xy = v. Hence solve the equation and show that y + 4x = 0 is a singular solution of the differential equation. (10)

**(b) [15 marks]**

A solid hemisphere is supported by a string fixed to a point on its rim and to a point on a smooth vertical wall with which the curved surface is in contact. If θ is the angle of inclination of the string with vertical and φ is the angle of inclination of the plane base of the hemisphere to the vertical, then find the value of (tan φ − tan θ).

**(c) [15 marks]**

If the tangent to a curve makes a constant angle θ with a fixed line, then prove that the ratio of radius of torsion to radius of curvature is proportional to tan θ. Further prove that if this ratio is constant, then the tangent makes a constant angle with a fixed direction.

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Solve the following initial value problem by using Laplace transform technique: d^{2}y/dt^{2} − 4 dy/dt + 3y(t) = f(t), y(0) = 1, y′(0) = 0 and f(t) is a given function of t.

**(b) [20 marks]**

A particle is projected from an apse at a distance √c from the centre of force with a velocity √((2λ/3)c^{3}) and is moving with central acceleration λ(r^{5} − c^{2}r). Find the path of motion of this particle. Will that be the curve x^{4} + y^{4} = c^{2}?

**(c) [15 marks]**

For a scalar point function φ and vector point function **f**, prove the identity ∇ · (φ**f**) = ∇φ · **f** + φ(∇ · **f**). Also find the value of ∇ · ((f(r)/r) **r**) and then verify stated identity.

## Paper II

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let G be a group of order 10 and G′ be a group of order 6. Examine whether there exists a homomorphism of G onto G′.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Express the ideal 4Z + 6Z as a principal ideal in the integral domain Z.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Test the convergence of the series ∑_{n=1}^{∞} [1.3.5 … (2n − 1)]/[2.4.6 … (2n)] · x^{2n+1}/(2n + 1), x > 0

**(d) [10 marks]**

State the sufficient conditions for a function f(z) = f(x + iy) = u(x, y) + iv(x, y) to be analytic in its domain. Hence, show that f(z) = log z is analytic in its domain and find df/dz.

**(e) [10 marks]**

A person requires 24, 24 and 20 units of chemicals A, B and C respectively for his garden. Product P contains 2, 4 and 1 units of chemicals A, B and C respectively per jar and product Q contains 2, 1 and 5 units of chemicals A, B and C respectively per jar. If a jar of P costs ₹ 30 and a jar of Q costs ₹ 50, then how many jars of each should be purchased in order to minimize the cost and meet the requirements?

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Prove that a non-commutative group of order 2p, where p is an odd prime, must have a subgroup of order p.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Using the method of Lagrange's multipliers, find the minimum and maximum distances of the point P(2, 6, 3) from the sphere x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} = 4.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Evaluate ∫_{0}^{2π} cos 2θ/(5 + 4 cos θ) dθ using contour integration.

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Prove that x^{2} + 1 is an irreducible polynomial in Z_{3}[x]. Further show that the quotient ring Z_{3}[x]/⟨x^{2} + 1⟩ is a field of 9 elements.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Prove that u(x, y) = e^{x}(x cos y − y sin y) is harmonic. Find its conjugate harmonic function v(x, y) and express the corresponding analytic function f(z) in terms of z.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Solve the following linear programming problem by Big M method:
Minimize Z = 2x_{1} + 3x_{2}
subject to
x_{1} + x_{2} ≥ 9
x_{1} + 2x_{2} ≥ 15
2x_{1} − 3x_{2} ≤ 9
x_{1}, x_{2} ≥ 0
Is the optimal solution unique? Justify your answer.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Prove that the oscillation of a real-valued bounded function f defined on [a, b] is the supremum of the set {|f(x_{1}) − f(x_{2})| : x_{1}, x_{2} ∈ [a, b]}.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Classify the singular point z = 0 of the function f(z) = e^{z}/(z − sin z) and obtain the principal part of its Laurent series expansion.

**(c) [20 marks]**

A department head has 5 subordinates and 5 jobs to be performed. The time (in hours) that each subordinate will take to perform each job is given in the matrix below:

| Subordinate | A | B | C | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | 4 | 9 | 4 | 12 | 4 |
| II | 15 | 11 | 20 | 5 | 8 |
| III | 17 | 7 | 15 | 12 | 18 |
| IV | 9 | 13 | 11 | 9 | 14 |
| V | 6 | 11 | 12 | 9 | 14 |
How should the jobs be assigned, one to each subordinate, so as to minimize the total time? Also, obtain the total minimum time to perform all the jobs if the subordinate IV cannot be assigned job C.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

By eliminating the arbitrary functions f and g from z = f(x^{2} − y) + g(x^{2} + y), form partial differential equation.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Given dy/dx = (y^{2} − x)/(y^{2} + x) with initial condition y = 1 at x = 0. Find the value of y for x = 0·4 by Euler's method, correct to 4 decimal places, taking step length h = 0·1.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Evaluate, using the binary arithmetic, the following numbers in their given system:
(i) (634·235)_{8} − (132·223)_{8}
(ii) (7AB·432)_{16} − (5CA·D61)_{16}

**(d) [10 marks]**

A planet of mass m is revolving around the sun of mass M. The kinetic energy T and the potential energy V of the planet are given by T = (1/2)m(ṙ^{2} + r^{2}θ̇^{2}) and V = GMm(1/(2a) − 1/r), where (r, θ) are the polar coordinates of the planet at time t, G is the gravitational constant and 2a is the major axis of the ellipse (the path of the planet). Find the Hamiltonian and the Hamilton equations of the planet's motion.

**(e) [10 marks]**

In a fluid motion, there is a source of strength 2m placed at z = 2 and two sinks of strength m are placed at z = 2 + i and z = 2 − i. Find the streamlines.

### Question 6

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the surface passing through the two lines z = x = 0 and z − 1 = x − y = 0, and satisfying the partial differential equation ∂^{2}z/∂x^{2} − 4 ∂^{2}z/∂x∂y + 4 ∂^{2}z/∂y^{2} = 0.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Solve the system of linear equations
7x_{1} − x_{2} + 2x_{3} = 11
2x_{1} + 8x_{2} − x_{3} = 9
x_{1} − 2x_{2} + 9x_{3} = 7
correct up to 4 significant figures by the Gauss-Seidel iterative method. Take initially guessed solution as x_{1} = x_{2} = x_{3} = 0.

**(c) [20 marks]**

A mechanical system with 2 degrees of freedom has the Lagrangian L = (1/2)m(ẋ^{2} + ẏ^{2}) − (1/2)m(w_{1}^{2}x^{2} + w_{2}^{2}y^{2}) + kxy where m, w_{1}, w_{2}, k are constants. Find the parameter θ so that under the transformation x = q_{1} cos θ − q_{2} sin θ, y = q_{1} sin θ + q_{2} cos θ the Lagrangian in terms of q_{1}, q_{2} will not contain the product term q_{1}q_{2}. Find the Lagrange's equations w.r.t. q_{1} and q_{2} independent of parameter θ.

## Paper I

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Can the set {(0, 0, 0, 3), (1, 1, 0, 0), (0, 1, −1, 0)} be extended to form a basis of the vector space ℝ^{4}? Justify your answer.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Find the range, rank, kernel and nullity of the linear transformation T : ℝ^{4} → ℝ^{3} given by T(x, y, z, w) = (x − w, y + z, z − w).

**(c) [10 marks]**

A rectangular sheet of metal of length 6 meters and width 2 meters is given. Four equal squares are removed from the four corners. The sides of this sheet are now folded up to form an open rectangular box. Find approximately the height of the box, such that the volume of the box is maximum.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Given that f(x + y) = f(x) f(y) for all real x, y, f(x) ≠ 0 for any real x and f′(0) = 2. Show that for all real x, f′(x) = 2f(x). Hence find f(x).

**(e) [10 marks]**

Find the equation of the cone whose vertex is the point (1, 1, 0) and whose guiding curve is y = 0, x^{2} + z^{2} = 4.

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Let T : ℝ^{3} → ℝ^{2} be a linear transformation such that T(1, 1, −1) = (1, 0), T(4, 1, 1) = (0, 1) and T(1, −1, 2) = (1, 1). Find T.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Using Mean Value Theorem, prove that
π/6 + √3/15 < sin^{−1}(3/5) < π/6 + 1/8.

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Find the equation of the cylinder whose generators are parallel to the line x/1 = y/2 = z/3 and that passes through the curve x^{2} + y^{2} = 16, z = 0. (10 marks)
(ii) Find the shortest distance between the straight lines (x − 3)/3 = (y − 8)/(−1) = (z − 3)/1 and (x + 3)/(−3) = (y + 7)/2 = (z − 6)/4. (10 marks)

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Reduce the following matrix to echelon form:
A = [[2, −2, 2, 1], [−3, 6, 0, −1], [1, −7, 10, 2]]

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the equations of the spheres which pass through the circle x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} − 2x + 2y + 4z − 3 = 0, 2x + y + z = 4 and touch the plane 3x + 4y = 14.

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Evaluate ∬_{R} y dx dy, where R is the region bounded by y = x and y = 4x − x^{2}. (10 marks)
(ii) If u(x, y) = x f(y/x) + g(y/x), where f and g are arbitrary functions, then show that
I. x ∂u/∂x + y ∂u/∂y = x f(y/x),
II. x^{2} ∂^{2}u/∂x^{2} + 2xy ∂^{2}u/∂x∂y + y^{2} ∂^{2}u/∂y^{2} = 0. (10 marks)

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Show that there is no tangent plane to the sphere
x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} − 4x + 2y − 4z + 4 = 0
that can be passed through the straight line
(x + 6)/2 = y + 3 = z + 1.

**(b) [15 marks]**

If f(x, y) = xy (x^{2} − y^{2})/(x^{2} + y^{2}), when (x, y) ≠ (0, 0)
f(x, y) = 0, when (x, y) = (0, 0),
then find f_{xy}(0, 0) and f_{yx}(0, 0).

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Find the eigenvalues and the corresponding eigenvectors of the matrix
A = [[1, 2, 0], [2, 1, −6], [2, −2, 3]]. (12 marks)
(ii) Let P_{n} denote the vector space of all polynomials of degree ≤ n over ℝ. Verify that
dim(P_{4}/P_{2}) = dim P_{4} − dim P_{2}. (8 marks)

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Solve (1 − y^{2} + y^{4}/x^{2})(dy/dx)^{2} − 2(y/x)(dy/dx) + y^{2}/x^{2} = 0.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Form the differential equation of all ellipses whose axes coincide with coordinate axes.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Prove that the time taken by the Earth to travel over half of its orbit, which is separated by the minor axis and is remote from the Sun, when the Sun is at the focus of the elliptic orbit, is two days more than half of the year. The eccentricity of the orbit is taken as 1/60.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Given that A and B are two points in the same horizontal line distant 2a apart. AO and BO are two equal heavy strings tied together at O and carrying their weight at O. If l is length of each string and d is depth of O below AB, then show that the parameter c of this catenary, in which the strings hang, is given by
l^{2} − d^{2} = 2c^{2}[cosh(a/c) − 1].

**(e) [10 marks]**

If u = x + y + z, v = x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} and w = xy + yz + zx, then show that grad u, grad v and grad w are coplanar.

### Question 6

**(a) [15 marks]**

If F(s) and G(s) are Laplace transforms of f(t) and g(t) respectively, then prove that 𝓛(∫_{0}^{t} f(x) g(t − x) dx) = F(s) G(s). Using this result, solve the equation y(t) = t + ∫_{0}^{t} y(x) sin(t − x) dx.

**(b) [15 marks]**

One end of an elastic string, having natural length a, is fixed at some point O and a heavy particle is attached to the other end of the string. The string is drawn vertically downward till it is four times its natural length at the point C and then released. If the modulus of elasticity of the string is equal to the weight of the particle, then show that the particle will return to the same point C in the time √(a/g) (2√3 + 4π/3).

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Find the absolute value of the directional derivative of φ(x, y, z) = x^{2}y^{2}z^{2} at the point (1, 1, −1) in the direction of the tangent to the curve x = e^{t}, y = 2 sin t + 1, z = t − cos t, at t = 0. (10 marks)
(ii) If ∇ · **E** = 0, ∇ · **H** = 0, ∇ × **E** = −∂**H**/∂t and ∇ × **H** = ∂**E**/∂t, then show that ∇^{2}**H** = ∂^{2}**H**/∂t^{2} and ∇^{2}**E** = ∂^{2}**E**/∂t^{2}. (10 marks)

### Question 7

**(a) [15 marks]**

A solid sphere rests inside a fixed rough and hemispherical bowl of twice its radius. If a large amount of weight, whatsoever, is attached to the highest point of the sphere, then show that the equilibrium is stable.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Verify Green's theorem in the plane for ∮_{C} [(xy + y^{2}) dx + x^{2} dy], where C is the boundary of the region bounded by the curves y = x and y = x^{2}.

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Find the general solution and singular solution of the differential equation (1 + dy/dx)^{3} = (27/8a)(x + y)(1 − dy/dx)^{3}. (10 marks)
(ii) Find the complete solution of x^{3} d^{3}y/dx^{3} + 3x^{2} d^{2}y/dx^{2} + x dy/dx + y = x log x. (10 marks)

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Solve the differential equation (x + 2) d^{2}y/dx^{2} − (2x + 5) dy/dx + 2y = (1 + x) e^{x} by the method of variation of parameters.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Verify Gauss's divergence theorem for **F** = [(x^{2} − yz) î + (y^{2} − zx) ĵ + (z^{2} − xy) k̂], taken over the rectangular parallelopiped 0 ≤ x ≤ a, 0 ≤ y ≤ b, 0 ≤ z ≤ c.

**(c) [20 marks]**

A particle is projected inside a fixed smooth cylinder with circular cross-section in a vertical plane from the lowest point with initial horizontal velocity u. Show that for
(i) (u^{2} ≤ 2ag); the particle oscillates about the mean position in the lower half,
(ii) (u^{2} ≥ 5ag); the particle executes complete circular motion, and
(iii) (2ag < u^{2} < 5ag); the particle will leave the curve in a tangential direction, making an angle α with the horizontal such that cos α = (u^{2} − 2ag)/3ag.

## Paper II

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let H and K be two subgroups of a group G such that o(H) > √o(G) and o(K) > √o(G). Show that H ∩ K ≠ {e}, where e is the identity element. Here o(H), o(K) and o(G) denote the order of H, K and G respectively.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Let G = {e, x, x^{2}, y, yx, yx^{2}} be a non-Abelian group with o(x) = 3 and o(y) = 2. Show that xy = yx^{2} (where e is the identity element of G and o(x), o(y) denote the order of the elements x, y respectively).

**(c) [10 marks]**

Examine whether the series ∑_{n=1}^{∞} (−1)^{n−1}/n is absolutely or conditionally convergent.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Expand f(z) = 1/[(z + 1)(z + 3)] in a Laurent series valid for 1 < |z| < 3.

**(e) [10 marks]**

How many basic solutions are there for the following system of equations?
2x_{1} − x_{2} + 3x_{3} + x_{4} = 6
4x_{1} − 2x_{2} − x_{3} + 2x_{4} = 10
Find all of them. Furthermore, find the number of basic solutions, which are feasible/non-feasible/non-degenerate.

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Define Cauchy sequence and prove that every convergent sequence of real numbers is a Cauchy sequence. What is the importance of Cauchy condition?

**(b) [15 marks]**

Show that 3 is an irreducible element in the integral domain Z[i].

**(c) [20 marks]**

Use the method of contour integration to prove that ∫_{−∞}^{∞} (x^{2} − x + 2)/(x^{4} + 10x^{2} + 9) dx = 5π/12.

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Evaluate the integral ∮_{C} e^{z}/[z^{2}(z + 1)^{3}] dz, C : |z| = 2.

**(b) [20 marks]**

Show that the volume of the greatest rectangular parallelopiped that can be inscribed in the ellipsoid x^{2}/a^{2} + y^{2}/b^{2} + z^{2}/c^{2} = 1 is 8abc/(3√3).

**(c) [15 marks]**

Apply the principle of duality to solve the following linear programming problem:
Maximize Z = 3x_{1} + 4x_{2}
subject to the constraints
x_{1} − x_{2} ≤ 1
x_{1} + x_{2} ≥ 4
x_{1} − 3x_{2} ≤ 3
x_{1}, x_{2} ≥ 0

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Examine whether the mapping φ : Z[x] → Z defined by φ(f(x)) = f(0), for f(x) ∈ Z[x], is a homomorphism. Deduce that the ideal ⟨x⟩ is a prime ideal in Z[x], but not a maximal ideal in Z[x].

**(b) [15 marks]**

Prove that every continuous function is Riemann integrable.

**(c) [20 marks]**

The following table shows all the necessary information on the available supply to each warehouse, the requirement of each market and the unit transportation cost from each warehouse to each market:

| Warehouse | I | II | III | IV | Supply |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 5 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 22 |
| B | 4 | 8 | 1 | 6 | 15 |
| C | 4 | 6 | 7 | 5 | 8 |
| Requirement | 7 | 12 | 17 | 9 | |

The shipping clerk has worked out the following schedule from experience:
12 units from A to II, 1 unit from A to III, 9 units from A to IV, 15 units from B to III, 7 units from C to I and 1 unit from C to III
Find the optimal schedule and minimum total shipping cost.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Find the solution of the equation (D^{2} + DD′ − 2D^{2}) z = y sin x, where D ≡ ∂/∂x and D′ ≡ ∂/∂y.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Solve the following system of linear equations by Gauss-Seidel method:
10x + 2y + z = 9
2x + 20y − 2z = −44
−2x + 3y + 10z = 22

**(c) [10 marks]**

(i) Convert the number (3479)_{10} into binary system and the number (7AE·9F)_{16} into decimal system.
(ii) Determine the truth table for the Boolean function
F(x, y, z) = (x + y + z′)(x′ + y′)
Also derive the full disjunctive normal form of F(x, y, z) from the truth table.

**(d) [10 marks]**

A bead of mass m slides on a frictionless wire in the shape of a cycloid given by x = a(θ − sin θ), y = a(1 + cos θ), (0 ≤ θ ≤ 2π). Find the Lagrangian function. Hence show that the equation of motion can be written as
d^{2}u/dt^{2} + (g/4a) u = 0
where u = cos(θ/2). (4 + 6 = 10 marks)

**(e) [10 marks]**

A source and a sink of equal strength are placed at points (±a/2, 0) within a fixed circular boundary x^{2} + y^{2} = a^{2}. Show that the streamlines are given by
(r^{2} − a^{2}/4)(r^{2} − 4a^{2}) − 4a^{2}y^{2} = ky(r^{2} − a^{2})
where k is a constant and r^{2} = x^{2} + y^{2}.

### Question 6

**(a) [20 marks]**

Solve ∂^{2}u/∂x^{2} + ∂^{2}u/∂y^{2} = 0 for a rectangular plate subject to the boundary conditions
u(0, y) = 0, u(a, y) = 0
u(x, 0) = 0, u(x, b) = f(x)

**(b) [15 marks]**

Simplify the Boolean function
F(x, y, z) = xyz + x′yz + xy′z + xyz′
and draw the corresponding GATE network.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Calculate the moment of inertia of a uniform solid cylinder of mass M, radius R and length L with respect to a set of axes passing through the centre of the cylinder, where z-axis is the axis of the cylinder and ρ is the constant density at any point of the cylinder. Also find L/R for which the moment of inertia about x- or y-axis will be minimum for a given mass of the cylinder.

### Question 7

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the complete integral of z(p^{2} − q^{2}) = x − y; p ≡ ∂z/∂x, q ≡ ∂z/∂y.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the unique polynomial of degree 2 or less which fits the following data:

| x | 0 | 1 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| f(x) | 1 | 3 | 55 |

Also obtain the bound on the truncation error.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Show that for an incompressible steady flow with constant viscosity, the velocity components
u(y) = (U/h) y − (hy/2μ)(dp/dx)(1 − y/h)
v = 0 = w, with p = p(x), satisfy the equation of motion in the absence of body force. Given that U, h and dp/dx are constants.

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Find the characteristics of the partial differential equation
p^{2} + q^{2} = 2; p ≡ ∂z/∂x, q ≡ ∂z/∂y
and determine the integral surface which passes through x = 0, z = y.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Find the constant p and error term for the quadrature formula
∫_{x_{0}}^{x_{1}} f(x) dx = (h/2)(f_{0} + f_{1}) + ph^{2}(f′_{0} − f′_{1})
where x_{0} + h = x_{1}, f_{0} = f(x_{0}), f_{1} = f(x_{1}) and prime (′) represents derivative with respect to x. Hence deduce the composite rule for integrating
∫_{a}^{b} f(x) dx, a = x_{0} < x_{1} < ⋯ < x_{N} = b

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) A particle of mass m moves in a force field of potential
V(r) = −k cos θ/r^{2}, k is constant
Find the Hamiltonian and the Hamilton's equations in spherical polar coordinates (r, θ, φ). (10 marks)
(ii) Consider the Lagrangian
L = m ẋ ẏ − mω_{0}^{2} xy
where m and ω_{0} are constants. Find the Hamiltonian and Hamilton's equations of motion. Identify the system. (10 marks)

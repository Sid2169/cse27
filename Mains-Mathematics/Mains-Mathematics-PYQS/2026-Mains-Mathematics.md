## Paper I

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Find all the values of *a* for which the system of homogeneous equations
ax + y + z = 0
x + y − z = 0
x + y − az = 0
has non-trivial solutions, and hence determine all the solutions for each value of *a*.

**(b) [10 marks]**

If A and B are similar matrices, then show that A and B have the same rank, trace, characteristic polynomial and eigenvalues.

**(c) [10 marks]**

If u(x, y, z) = (x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2})^{−1/2}, then find the value of ∂^{2}u/∂x^{2} + ∂^{2}u/∂y^{2} + ∂^{2}u/∂z^{2}.

**(d) [10 marks]**

Using Maclaurin's theorem, obtain the first four terms of the expansion of e^{x cos x} in ascending powers of x. Hence or otherwise find the limit of (e^{x} − e^{x cos x})/(x − sin x) as x tends to zero.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Obtain the equation of the sphere having its centre on the line 5y + 2z = 0 = 2x − 3y and passing through the points (0, −2, −4) and (2, −1, −1).

### Question 2

**(a) [20 marks]**

Let T : P_{2}[x] → P_{2}[x] be a linear transformation defined by
T(a_{0} + a_{1}x + a_{2}x^{2}) = −(a_{0} + 2a_{1} + a_{2}) + (2a_{0} + 3a_{1})x − 2(a_{0} + a_{1})x^{2}
where P_{2}[x] denotes the set of all polynomials in x of degree ≤ 2 and a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2} ∈ ℝ. Find the eigenvalues and corresponding eigenvectors of T using the matrix representation of T with respect to the standard basis of P_{2}[x].

**(b) [15 marks]**

If the sum of the lengths of the hypotenuse and another side of a right-angled triangle be given, then find the angle between these sides so that the area of the triangle is maximum.

**(c) [15 marks]**

Find the points on the lines (x − 3)/1 = (y − 5)/(−2) = (z − 7)/1 and (x + 1)/7 = (y + 1)/(−6) = (z + 1)/1 which are nearest to each other. Hence find the shortest distance between the lines and its equation.

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Given P_{3}[x] = {a_{0} + a_{1}x + a_{2}x^{2} + a_{3}x^{3} | a_{0}, a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3} ∈ ℝ} as a vector space over the field ℝ.
(i) Let H = {p(x) ∈ P_{3}[x] | ∫_{−1}^{1} p(x) dx = 0}. Prove that H is a subspace of P_{3}[x] and find a basis of H.
(ii) Find a subspace K of P_{3}[x] such that P_{3}[x] = H ⊕ K. (8 + 7 = 15)

**(b) [20 marks]**

(i) Evaluate the double integral ∫_{0}^{2} ∫_{x^{2}+1}^{2x+1} x^{2}y dy dx by reversing the order of integration. (10)
(ii) Find the volume of the solid, which is formed from the intersection of the coordinate planes x = 0, y = 0, z = 0 and the plane x/a + y/b + z/c = 1. (10)

**(c) [15 marks]**

If x/1 = y/2 = z/3 represents one of a set of three mutually perpendicular generators of the cone 5yz − 8zx − 3xy = 0, then find the equations of the other two.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Let F be a subfield of complex numbers and let T be the linear transformation from F^{3} into F^{3}, defined by
T(x, y, z) = (x − y + 2z, 2x + y, −x − 2y + 2z)
If (a, b, c) is a vector in F^{3}, then answer the following:
(i) Find the condition on a, b, c such that the vector (a, b, c) be in the null space of T. What is the nullity of T?
(ii) Find the condition on a, b, c such that the vector (a, b, c) be in the range of T. What is the rank of T? (8 + 7 = 15)

**(b) [15 marks]**

Trace the curve y^{2}(a + x) = x^{2}(3a − x).

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Find the equations of the generating lines of the hyperboloid x^{2}/4 + y^{2}/9 − z^{2}/16 = 1 which pass through the points (2, 3, −4) and (2, −1, 4/3). (10)
(ii) Find the equation of the right circular cylinder whose axis is x = 2y = −z, and radius is 4. Prove that the area of the section of this cylinder by the plane z = 0 is 24π. (10)

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

If y_{1} and y_{2} are two solutions of dy/dx + P(x)y = Q(x), and y_{2} = y_{1}u, then show that u = 1 + a e^{−∫(Q/y_{1}) dx}, where a is a non-zero constant.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Apply Laplace transform to solve (D^{2} + D) y(t) = 2, D ≡ d/dt, subject to the conditions y(0) = 3, y′(0) = 1.

**(c) [10 marks]**

A smooth paraboloid of revolution is fixed with its axis vertical and vertex upwards; on it is placed a heavy elastic string of unstretched length 2πb. When the string is in equilibrium, apply the principle of virtual work to show that it rests in the form of a circle of radius 4πabλ/(4πaλ − Wb), where W is the weight of the string, λ is the modulus of elasticity and 4a is the latus rectum of the generating parabola.

**(d) [10 marks]**

A particle falls from rest at the vertex of an inverted catenary. Prove that the particle will leave the curve when the path described is √3 times the vertical distance through which the particle has fallen.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Given **F** = (2x + 3y) î − 4z ĵ − 5x k̂ and S is the surface 2x + 4y + 4z = 5, bounded by x = 0, x = 2, y = 0 and y = 3. Evaluate ∬_{S} (∇ × **F**) · n̂ dS.

### Question 6

**(a) [15 marks]**

Solve x^{2} d^{2}y/dx^{2} − 3x dy/dx + y = ((sin(log x) + 1)/x) log x.

**(b) [20 marks]**

(i) A battleship is steaming ahead with velocity u. A gun is mounted on the ship so as to point straight backwards and is set at an angle of elevation α. If v be the velocity of projection relative to the gun, show that the range is (2v/g) sin α (v cos α − u) and the angle for maximum range is cos^{−1}((u + √(u^{2} + 8v^{2}))/(4v)), where g stands for acceleration due to gravity. (10)
(ii) A uniform chain of length l has one end fixed at a height h above a rough table and rests in a vertical plane so that a portion of it lies in a straight line on the table. Prove that if the chain is on the point of slipping, the length on the table is (l + μh) − √((μ^{2} + 1)h^{2} + 2μlh), where μ is the coefficient of friction. (10)

**(c) [15 marks]**

Determine T̂, N̂, B̂, κ and τ for the parabola y^{2} = 4ax, where a is a constant. Here T̂, N̂, B̂, κ and τ denote unit tangent vector, unit principal normal vector, unit binormal vector, curvature and torsion respectively.

### Question 7

**(a) [15 marks]**

A light elastic string of natural length l has one extremity fixed at a point A and the other attached to a stone (mass m) the weight of which in equilibrium would extend the string to a length b. Show that if the stone be dropped from rest at A, it will come to instantaneous rest at a depth √(b^{2} − l^{2}) below the equilibrium position and this depth is attained in time √(2l/g) + √((b − l)/g) {π − cos^{−1} √((b − l)/(b + l))}. Prove also that if the greatest depth below A be l cot^{2}(θ/2), then the modulus of elasticity is (1/2) mg tan^{2} θ.

**(b) [15 marks]**

If **F** = 4xz î − y^{2} ĵ + yz k̂, then evaluate ∬_{S} **F** · n̂ dS, where S is the surface of a unit cube with two opposite corners at (0, 0, 0) and (1, 1, 1) respectively. Hence verify the divergence theorem.

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) Show that (x + y + 1)^{−4} is an integrating factor of (2x − y − 1)y dx + (2y − x − 1)x dy = 0. Hence solve it. (10)
(ii) If L{f(t)} = f̄(s), then show that L{f(t)/t} = ∫_{s}^{∞} f̄(u) du. Hence evaluate L{(cos at − cos bt)/t}. (10)

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Solve d^{2}y/dx^{2} + a^{2}y = sec ax by the method of variation of parameters.

**(b) [15 marks]**

AB is a uniform rod of length l and weight W, which can turn freely about a fixed point in its length distant l/3 from A. AC and BC are light strings each of length (5/6)l, attached to a particle C of weight w. Prove that if W is less than 2w, there will be stable equilibrium with AB inclined to the horizontal at an angle tan^{−1}((W + w)/(4w)).

**(c) [20 marks]**

(i) For a scalar point function φ and a vector point function **F**, prove that ∇ × (φ**F**) = (∇φ) × **F** + φ(∇ × **F**). If **r** = x î + y ĵ + z k̂ and r = |**r**|, then verify the above identity for φ = 1/r and **F** = **r**. (10)
(ii) If **r** = a cos t î + a sin t ĵ + bt k̂, then show that |**r**′ × **r**″|^{2} = a^{2}(a^{2} + b^{2}) and [**r**′ **r**″ **r**‴] = a^{2}b. (10)

## Paper II

### Question 1

**(a) [10 marks]**

Let G be a group of order pq, where p and q are primes. Show that either G is an abelian group or no non-identity element commutes with every element of G.

**(b) [10 marks]**

Show that every non-zero prime ideal in a Euclidean Domain is maximal.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Find the points of discontinuity of the function f : (0, 1) → ℝ defined by f(x) = lim_{n→∞} [(1 + sin(π/x))^{n} − 1]/[(1 + sin(π/x))^{n} + 1], x ∈ (0, 1).

**(d) [10 marks]**

Show that the function f(z) = √|xy| is not analytic at the origin, although Cauchy-Riemann equations are satisfied at that point.

**(e) [10 marks]**

Solve the dual problem of the following linear programming problem by the graphical method:
Minimize Y = 10y_{1} + 6y_{2} + 2y_{3} + y_{4}
subject to the constraints:
y_{1} + y_{2} + y_{3} + y_{4} ≥ 2
2y_{1} + y_{2} − y_{3} − 2y_{4} ≥ 1
y_{1}, y_{2}, y_{3}, y_{4} ≥ 0

### Question 2

**(a) [15 marks]**

Let G be a finite group generated by two elements u and v of order 2 each. Show that G is isomorphic to the dihedral group of order 2n, where O(uv) = n.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Prove that the sequence (u_{n}) defined by the recursion formula u_{n+1} = √(7 + u_{n}), u_{1} = √7, converges to the positive root of x^{2} − x − 7 = 0.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Use the method of contour integration to prove ∫_{0}^{2π} sin^{2}θ/(a + b cos θ) dθ = (2π/b^{2}){a − √(a^{2} − b^{2})}, if a > b > 0.

### Question 3

**(a) [15 marks]**

Prove that cosh(z + 1/z) = a_{0} + ∑_{n=1}^{∞} a_{n}(z^{n} + 1/z^{n}), where a_{n} = (1/(2π)) ∫_{0}^{2π} cos nθ cosh(2 cos θ) dθ, for n = 0, 1, 2, ….

**(b) [20 marks]**

A function f is defined on [0, 1] by
f(x) = x^{2}, when x is rational;
f(x) = x^{3}, when x is irrational.
Evaluate the lower integral ∫_{0}^{1} f and the upper integral ∫_{0}^{1} f. Does the integral ∫_{0}^{1} f exist?

**(c) [15 marks]**

The following table represents the estimated costs of assigning jobs to machines. Solve this assignment problem to minimize the total cost.

| Job | M_{1} | M_{2} | M_{3} | M_{4} |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J_{1} | 8 | 24 | 28 | 32 |
| J_{2} | 8 | 13 | 17 | 19 |
| J_{3} | 8 | 15 | 19 | 22 |

Is the assignment unique for the total minimum cost? Justify your answer and obtain an alternate assignment, if it exists.

### Question 4

**(a) [15 marks]**

Let n be a positive integer. Show that the following statements are equivalent:
(i) ℤ/nℤ is an integral domain.
(ii) ℤ/nℤ is a field.
(iii) n is prime.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Prove that the improper integral ∫_{0}^{∞} dx/(1 + x^{2} sin^{2}x) is divergent.

**(c) [20 marks]**

Solve the following linear programming problem by the Simplex method:
Maximize Z = 4x_{1} + 10x_{2}
subject to the constraints
2x_{1} + x_{2} ≤ 10
2x_{1} + 5x_{2} ≤ 20
2x_{1} + 3x_{2} ≤ 18
x_{1}, x_{2} ≥ 0
Obtain an alternate optimal solution, if it exists, with explanation.

### Question 5

**(a) [10 marks]**

Find the integral surface of the equations dx/(xz − y) = dy/(yz − x) = dz/(1 − z^{2}).

**(b) [10 marks]**

By using the Newton-Raphson iterative formula, establish the formula x_{i+1} = (1/3)(2x_{i} + N/x_{i}^{2}) to find the cube root of N. Use this formula, if established, to find the cube root of 63 correct to four decimal places with the initial approximation 3·9.

**(c) [10 marks]**

Consider the logic circuit L:
[Figure: inputs A, B, C feed three AND gates whose outputs go to one OR gate with output Y. First AND gate: inputs A, B, C. Second AND gate: inputs A, NOT B (through an inverter), C. Third AND gate: inputs NOT A (through an inverter), B.]
(i) Express Y as a Boolean expression.
(ii) Write 8-bit distinct sequences for A, B and C.
(iii) Find the truth table of L using the 8-bit distinct sequences.

**(d) [10 marks]**

A simple source of strength m is fixed at the origin O in a uniform stream of incompressible fluid moving with velocity U î. Find out the velocity potential φ at any point P of the stream where OP = r and θ is the angle **OP** makes with the direction î. Find the differential equation of the stream lines and show that they lie on the surface Ur^{2} sin^{2}θ − 2m cos θ = constant.

**(e) [10 marks]**

A uniform heavy solid hemisphere of radius 'a' is held at rest with its base vertical and its curved surface in contact with a horizontal plane. If the hemisphere is released when the plane is rough enough to prevent slipping, show that the angle θ that the base makes with the horizontal at time t, is such that (dθ/dt)^{2} = 15g cos θ/(a(28 − 15 cos θ)).

### Question 6

**(a) [20 marks]**

Reduce the partial differential equation y ∂^{2}z/∂x^{2} + (x + y) ∂^{2}z/∂x∂y + x ∂^{2}z/∂y^{2} = 0 to canonical form and hence solve it.

**(b) [15 marks]**

(i) Find the decimal equivalent of the following floating point 10-bit numbers with 5 bits as fractional part: 1010111011 and 0100101010. (8)
(ii) Evaluate (11001.1011)_{10} + (33.24)_{8} + (101101)_{2} and convert the final result in hexadecimal system. (7)

**(c) [15 marks]**

Suppose that there is an infinitely long cylinder of radius 'a' placed in a uniform stream having velocity −V î. Then using Milne-Thomson's circle theorem, what will be its complex velocity potential? If in addition, a circulation round the cylinder of k is produced, find out the stagnation points. Do they produce a lifting tendency in the vertical direction? Explain how.

### Question 7

**(a) [15 marks]**

Solve the partial differential equation (2D^{2} − 5DD′ + 2D′^{2}) z = 24(y − x) + sin(y − x), where D ≡ ∂/∂x, D′ ≡ ∂/∂y, z = z(x, y).

**(b) [15 marks]**

Evaluate the integral ∫_{0}^{0·8} f(x) dx, where f(x) = 0·2 + 25x − 200x^{2} + 675x^{3} − 900x^{4} + 400x^{5} by using single application of Simpson's 3/8 rule.

**(c) [20 marks]**

A smooth uniform rod, say OA, of length 2a and mass m is pivoted at one end to a fixed point O. The rod is inclined at an angle θ with the downward vertical line OZ and the plane OAZ makes an angle φ with a fixed vertical plane. A bead of mass λm slides smoothly on the rod and is connected to O by a light elastic string of modulus nmg and natural length a. Show that the kinetic energy T of the system is given by 2T = (4/3)ma^{2}(θ̇^{2} + φ̇^{2} sin θ) + λm(ẋ^{2} + x^{2}θ̇^{2} + x^{2}θ̇^{2} sin^{2}θ), where x is the stretched length of the string, and derive the equations of motion.

### Question 8

**(a) [15 marks]**

Two-dimensional harmonic equation in plane polar coordinates (r, θ) takes the form:
∂^{2}V/∂r^{2} + (1/r) ∂V/∂r + (1/r^{2}) ∂^{2}V/∂θ^{2} = 0
Deduce that it has solutions of the form (Ar^{n} + Br^{−n}) e^{±inθ}, where A, B, n are constants. Determine V if it satisfies the two-dimensional harmonic equation in the region 0 ≤ r ≤ a, 0 ≤ θ ≤ 2π and satisfies the conditions:
(i) V remains finite as r → 0,
(ii) V = ∑_{n} c_{n} cos(nθ) on r = a.

**(b) [15 marks]**

Consider the initial value problem: dy/dx = 1 + y/x, y(1) = 1, h = 1 (Step-size)
(i) Apply the classical fourth order Runge-Kutta method to approximate the solution y(2).
(ii) Use Euler's method to find y(2) and y(3).

**(c) [20 marks]**

A viscous incompressible fluid is filled between two concentric cylinders of radius a, b (b > a). The flow is steady and no body forces are taken into consideration. Discuss and formulate the velocity of the fluid if:
(i) the inner cylinder is given angular velocity Ω while the outer one is held at rest;
(ii) the outer cylinder is rotated with angular velocity Ω while the inner one is held at rest.

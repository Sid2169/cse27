# 023. Constitutional structure, citizenship and change

**Paper:** Prelims General Studies I, UPSC CSE 2027.
**Learning outcome:** Locate a provision and distinguish ordinary legislation from constitutional amendment.

**Status:** Authored teaching chapter in three parts, 24 November 2026.

**Curriculum entry:** [Chapter 023](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Curriculum.md#chapter-023), in [State, democracy and the Constitution](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Curriculum.md#topic-political-foundations-and-constitution-making).

**Scope:** Parts, articles and schedules; the Union and its territory; citizenship; official languages; constitutional amendment and basic structure. This addresses the Constitution and political-system component of the [official paper syllabus](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Syllabus.md).

**Prerequisites:** Complete [Chapter 022 — Political foundations and constitution-making](022-political-foundations-and-constitution-making.md). You need its distinction between the State and government, constitutional limits and ordinary political decisions. Basic reading, chronology and percentage skills suffice; no additional civics knowledge is assumed. The curriculum's Chapter 004 remains a planning placeholder, so numerical reasoning needed for amendment majorities is taught here.

<a id="summary-of-key-concepts-and-takeaways"></a>

## 1. Summary of key concepts and takeaways

A constitutional claim must be matched to its operative provision, legal category and date. The three parts connect document reading and territorial authority to citizenship/language rules, then distinguish an authorised amendment procedure from constitutional validity. Schedule changes, statutory changes and Article 368 amendments do not all use the same route.

**Paper:** Prelims General Studies I, UPSC CSE 2027.
**Learning outcome:** Identify the right, applicable limit and possible remedy in a simple situation.

**Status:** Authored teaching chapter in three parts, 24 November 2026.

**Curriculum entry:** [Chapter 024](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Curriculum.md#chapter-024), within [Rights, duties and Directive Principles](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Curriculum.md#topic-fundamental-rights-and-constitutional-remedies).

<a id="summary-of-key-concepts-and-takeaways"></a>

## 1. Summary of key concepts and takeaways

Read each guarantee through **actor, beneficiary, right, limit and remedy**. Equality can accommodate relevant differences; liberty requires valid limits and fair procedure; religious and minority rights depend on their particular wording. A court remedy follows the legal problem and jurisdiction, not simply the fact that someone alleges unfairness.

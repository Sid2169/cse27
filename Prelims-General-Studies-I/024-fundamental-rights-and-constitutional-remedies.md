# 024. Fundamental Rights and constitutional remedies

**Paper:** Prelims General Studies I, UPSC CSE 2027.

**Status:** Authored teaching chapter in three parts, 24 November 2026.

**Curriculum entry:** [Chapter 024](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Curriculum.md#chapter-024), within [Rights, duties and Directive Principles](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Curriculum.md#topic-fundamental-rights-and-constitutional-remedies).

**Prerequisites:** [Chapter 022](022-political-foundations-and-constitution-making.md) for constitutional limits and [Chapter 023](023-constitutional-structure-citizenship-and-change.md) for articles, clauses, citizenship and judicial review of amendments. No legal training is assumed.

**Scope:** Fundamental Rights, their beneficiaries and limits; equality and liberty; protection against exploitation; religious, cultural and educational rights; writs and enforcement. This addresses the Constitution and rights-issues components of the [official Prelims GS-I syllabus](Prelims-General-Studies-I-Syllabus.md). Detailed Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties belong to Chapter 025, not this chapter.

| Part | Content | Practical outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [1 — Foundations and equality](024-fundamental-rights-and-constitutional-remedies-part-1.md) | Articles 12–18; State action, beneficiaries, equality and enabling safeguards | Identify who can invoke a right, against whom, and why different treatment may be constitutionally justified |
| [2 — Freedom, personal liberty and protection from exploitation](024-fundamental-rights-and-constitutional-remedies-part-2.md) | Articles 19–24; restrictions, criminal safeguards, privacy, education and detention | Read the particular entitlement together with its particular limits |
| [3 — Religion, culture and remedies](024-fundamental-rights-and-constitutional-remedies-part-3.md) | Articles 25–35; Articles 32/226, five writs, property and emergency qualifications | Connect a simple infringement to an appropriate constitutional remedy |

Read the three parts in order. Together they constitute one curriculum chapter. Examples are invented teaching situations unless identified as judicial decisions or historical PYQs. Sources and access dates are provided in each part.

**Corpus discrepancy:** Part 3 flags an incorrect recorded key for local **2022 Q87**, concerning mandamus and quo warranto. The original question corpus is preserved; the chapter supplies an explicitly reasoned correction.

## 1. Summary of key concepts and takeaways

The President's election balances elected Union and Assembly representation through weighted votes; the Vice-President's election uses both Houses' elected and nominated members with equal votes. Election, removal and succession each use their own rules. The Vice-President is not automatically elected President when temporarily performing presidential functions.

Presidential authority operates within responsible parliamentary government. An appointment, assent decision, ordinance or mercy order must be understood through its article, required circumstances, advice framework and legal limits. The Vice-President's Rajya Sabha role and the President's executive role remain distinct even when one person temporarily performs the other's functions.

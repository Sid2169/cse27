## Chapter exam simulations — 27 November 2026

- Ready chapters and their parts open the same whole-chapter exam simulation from the reader toolbar. Chapter practice includes timed Prelims MCQs and written Mains questions, retained answer files and transcription review, frozen feedback, and chapter-specific history.
- Qualified current-revision attempts update revision due dates with the default scheduling settings. Refresher recommendations are retained with the result. The revision view exposes schedule preferences, overrides and task history.

- Finalized Mains chapter simulations can show their stored reference solutions on request from feedback. Active attempts have no access to model answers.

- The combined refresher checklist supports on-demand tests. Questions, guides and answers remain transient in the application. A passing result updates only the checklist completion flag.

- Cumulative exams use a snapshot of currently qualified chapter revisions, with paper-specific marking and no change to chapter revision dates. The dashboard shows the latest cumulative percentage per paper variant. It also lists unfinished exams, pending refresher checklists and revisions due today.

- The full-scope CSAT diagnostic uses the 2026 pattern (80 questions, 200 marks, two hours), verifies batches, and compares drafts with previously presented paper questions. Dashboard performance graphs use only full-scope scores. Mathematics I and II have separate graphs, and paper cards show the latest full-scope score.

- Revision preferences configure saved timezone, default material, difficulty and priority, and stage-specific targets. The revision view groups overdue, due-today, upcoming and unscheduled tasks. A month calendar shows scheduled days and lets learners open that day’s assessments. Each qualified chapter also accepts overrides for material type, difficulty, priority and target date. Automatic schedules preserve their previous dates in history when settings change, and recorded exam scheduling decisions include the applied settings. Manual dates, cancellations and recent task history are available in the revision view.

- The official past-paper selector displays repository transcription caveats and opens verified original booklets or UPSC’s listings. 2024 CSAT Series A pairs the unabridged original booklet with a timed answer sheet and the published UPSC key. 2024 Prelims GS-I Series A also has a timed, scored answer sheet. The three official dropped items remain in the booklet and are excluded from scoring. The 2024 GS-I Series A paper supports an on-demand 2026 updated variant. Replacements are one for one, checked against a dated source pack and independently solved before publishing. The attempt links directly to the original booklet for the unchanged questions. Both 2023 and both 2025 Prelims papers support scored Series A attempts. The 2023 GS-I key drops one question. Both 2026 Prelims papers have Series A practice scores using UPSC’s provisional keys, explicitly marked provisional. Historical written attempts open the original PDFs and prepare private practice rubrics before accepting an answer. Supported editions are 2026 Essay, GS I–IV and both Mathematics papers, plus Hindi and English in both 2024 and 2026. The selector links directly to the official PDF for every supported paper from 2016–2026 using UPSC’s archive. Scoring additional historical editions requires matching keys or verified practice patterns.

- Newly finalized full-scope and written historical attempts can use their frozen, question-specific feedback as refresher study material when chapter teaching is unavailable. Updated PYQs can use generated replacement feedback. Official answer-sheet-only items still require suitable authored teaching material.

- Encrypted backup and restore include ordinary simulations, answer files, frozen feedback, revision data and the refresher completion flag. Transient test content is outside the backup.

- The full-scope GS-I diagnostic uses 100 questions, 200 marks and two hours. It covers the seven syllabus areas and records a dated official source pack for changing facts. The source pack must be maintained as coverage moves forward.

- Full-scope Mains GS I–III diagnostics use 20 compulsory questions, 250 marks and three hours, with separate 150- and 250-word sections. GS IV follows the theory-subpart and case-study structure. Essay offers two sections of four topics and marks one chosen topic from each. Hindi Paper A and English Paper B follow the verified 2026 qualifying-paper structure, with one short essay, compulsory language exercises and a 75/300 practice benchmark. Mathematics Papers I and II use separate syllabi and each requires Questions 1 and 5 plus three selections, with at least one selection from each section.

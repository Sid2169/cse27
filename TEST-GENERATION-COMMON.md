# Shared Test Generation

Use with the paper-specific Prelims or Mains generator. [SPECIFICATION.md](SPECIFICATION.md) owns product requirements; [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) owns shared safeguards. This file owns cross-paper generation procedures, not scoring, chapter qualification or scheduling.

## Mode and Coverage

| Mode | Procedure |
|---|---|
| `CH` | Read the indexed chapter and all parts; sample its important taught ideas and skills. Choose a worthwhile size without padding. |
| `CUM` | Read the learner's checkpoint-qualified chapter list. Scale questions, marks and duration to that coverage; record the chapters actually assessed. |
| `FULL` | Use the whole syllabus and verified paper pattern. Check unseen status below; label early attempts diagnostic. |
| `PYQ` / `UPYQ` | Prepare the selected historical paper using the procedure below. |
| `FU` | Read all refresher recommendations in one real-paper simulation's feedback. Generate one transient test covering them together, using the specification's count and paper-appropriate formats; prepare a key/guide. |

CH/CUM require substantive teaching material; placeholders are not evidence of readiness. Supply unfamiliar context needed to apply taught knowledge. Explain insufficient scope rather than silently changing modes. FULL/PYQ may precede teaching completion. Checkpoints use [CHAPTER-CHECKPOINT.md](CHAPTER-CHECKPOINT.md).

## Unseen Questions

For FULL, compare drafts with retained simulation/checkpoint history, including close rewrites; change the tested scenario or construction meaningfully, not just names or numbers. Concepts may recur. Replace duplicates before presentation. Transient feedback to-do questions are outside this history and are not saved for comparison.

## Original and Updated PYQs

1. Identify the year, paper, booklet/language where relevant and reliable source. Check completeness, instructions, passages, figures and numbering against the official paper. If a transcription is inadequate, provide the official paper rather than reconstruct missing content.
2. For PYQ, preserve the complete original. Prefer the available official objective key, matched to the correct booklet; label independently prepared keys/guides as practice interpretations.
3. For UPYQ, retain a separate original. Identify questions whose original purpose depended on contemporary affairs; historical references alone do not make a question replaceable. For each replacement, identify the exact aspect tested and seek its target-year counterpart under the specification's precise-mirroring rule. Use its new-question fallback only when meaningful mirroring is unavailable.
4. Validate replacements and their keys/guides independently against identifiable, dated sources. Explain evidence gaps rather than invent future events/provisions. Record source-question mapping, mirrored/new status, rationale, target year, cutoff and supporting sources for post-attempt feedback. Preserve unchanged questions and the source paper's structure and choice rules.

Use the paper generator for question design and keys/guides, and its evaluation method for scoring. To-do-test completion and non-persistence remain owned by the specification; only chapter tests enter [revision scheduling](REVISION-SCHEDULING.md).

Process FU only transiently and discard its working data after use. Do not create repository test files or durable jobs containing FU questions, guides, submissions or evaluations.
